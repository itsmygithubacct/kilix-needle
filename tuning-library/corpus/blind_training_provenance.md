# Blind training additions

Authored for the `kilix_panes` training corpus as fresh examples from the tool contract and generator format. These templates were composed without reading evaluation sets, heldout data, logs, conversations, or any existing off-domain list. The off-domain request strings are independently authored broad examples of tasks outside pane and tab control. They are intended as refusal training inputs, not as a canonical catalog of user language.

New action template files: `actions/maximize_pane_blind.json`, `actions/rename_pane_blind.json`, `actions/swap_panes_blind.json`, and `actions/move_tab_blind.json`. Off-domain requests: `off_domain_blind.json`.
